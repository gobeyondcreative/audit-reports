Methodology and Data Limitations

Scoring:

The headline score and section scores are taken directly from the Sep 8, 2026 Boostcon App audit: Overall 56%, Business Details 60%, Tech Stack 20%, Google Business Profile 100%, Listings 36%, Reputation 85%, Website Performance 77%, and Local SEO Heatmaps 16%. The previous custom 27 out of 100 score was removed because it repeatedly penalized the same assumptions across categories, and several assumptions were contradicted by current evidence.

Evidence reconciliation:

The Boostcon App is used for captured diagnostics. Current website and public business data are used to verify whether findings are still live. When sources disagree, the discrepancy is reported instead of silently selecting the more dramatic number. Review counts and local ranks change over time.

Revenue claims:

No specific lost-revenue estimate is presented. Without verified call volume, lead volume, close rate, average ticket, booked-job rate, and source-level attribution, a precise revenue-loss number is not defensible.

Limitations:

The exact Colorado Secretary of State registration, founding date, total revenue, and employee count were not independently confirmed. The Google / local business phone may be an intentional tracking line and should be verified by the company. Social profiles may exist without being indexed or confidently attributable. Ad and tracking technologies are described as detected or not detected at audit time, not as permanent states.

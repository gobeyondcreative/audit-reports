Executive Summary. All Year Cooling & Plumbing (legal: All Year Cooling and Heating, Inc.) is a 52 year old family business with a strong brand foundation: 4.7 stars across ~2,600 Google reviews, an A+ BBB accreditation, and a large well structured website with 269 pages and 80+ city level landing pages. Their conversion architecture (click to call, SMS, booking, coupons, financing) is above average for the HVAC category.

However, their digital presence has three structural weaknesses costing them market share. First, they rank #9 on Google Maps for "air conditioning contractor near me" in their own market (per GHL heatmap), while competitors Art Plumbing (10,600+ reviews) and Snyder AC (29,000+ reviews) dominate. Second, their NAP consistency is broken with 5+ different phone numbers across Google, Yelp, BBB, Facebook, and their own website. GHL confirms only 52% of their 31 checked citations are accurate. Third, their website performance is catastrophic: 18.2 second mobile load time (GHL data) with Core Web Vitals near zero.

The composite Online Health Score is 58 out of 100. The biggest immediate opportunity is fixing website speed and local SEO (NAP standardization + organic ranking) to capture high intent search traffic they are currently losing to competitors on the same street.

05 Top 5 Immediate Opportunities

Opportunity 01

Fix Website Speed and Core Web Vitals (Emergency)

GHL confirmed 18.2 second mobile load time with Core Web Vitals at 0 to 6%. This is actively suppressing Google rankings. No amount of content or backlinks will help if the site is this slow. Compress images, remove duplicated carousels, minify assets, implement caching. This is the highest priority fix because it is a prerequisite for all other SEO work.

Impact: Critical | Timeline: 1 to 2 weeks
Opportunity 02

Standardize NAP and Fix Local SEO Foundation

Consolidate to one primary phone number across all 31+ directories. GHL confirms 48% of citations are wrong. Once citations are clean, their 2,580+ reviews and 52 year history become a much stronger ranking signal. This directly impacts their #9 map pack ranking.

Impact: High | Timeline: 2 to 4 weeks
Opportunity 03

Capture Page 1 for Core Coral Springs HVAC Keywords

They have a dedicated Coral Springs landing page that is indexed but not ranking. With speed fixed and NAP clean, optimize for "AC repair Coral Springs", "HVAC Coral Springs", "AC installation Coral Springs". Competitors Art Plumbing (#8) and Snyder (#2) are beatable on content quality if the technical foundation is fixed.

Impact: High | Timeline: 3 to 6 months
Opportunity 04

Deploy Systematic Review Generation to Close the Review Gap

AYR has ~2,580 Google reviews. Snyder has 29,000 total. Art has 10,560. Review volume is a primary local SEO and trust signal. Implement post service SMS review requests, GBP review link in email signatures, tech driven review asks. Target 50+ per month. This is the long game moat that compounds.

Impact: High | Timeline: Ongoing
Opportunity 05

Add 24/7 AI Chat Widget to Capture After Hours Leads

Their own negative reviews repeatedly mention phones not being answered and callbacks not returned. GHL confirms no chat widget. A 24/7 AI chat captures leads when humans cannot, books appointments, and filters urgent vs non urgent requests. Directly addresses their biggest reputation weakness while adding a new lead channel.

Impact: Medium High | Timeline: 1 to 2 weeks

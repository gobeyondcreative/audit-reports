Executive Summary. All Year Cooling & Plumbing (legal: All Year Cooling and Heating, Inc.) is a 52 year old South Florida HVAC company doing ~$19M/year. Acquired March 2024 by Creative Service Partners (Trivest Partners backed, ~$6.1B AUM). Sister companies: Hammond Services, Besel Plumbing, The Clean Plumbers. Tommy Smith is the public face but the buying committee is a PE roll up sponsor. Site still claims "Family Owned & Operated Since 1973" which is now misleading.

Brand foundation is strong: 4.8 stars, ~2,536 Google reviews, A+ BBB since 2007, 738 URL website. But five critical failures are costing market share: (1) GTM disabled sitewide (HTML commented out), no Google Ads conversion tag, spending blind on Google + Microsoft + Meta. (2) 167 of 276 location URLs redirect (60.5%) while still in sitemap. City+service layer collapsed. (3) Review schema fabricated: hardcoded 5.0/100 vs real 4.8/2,536. Manual action risk. (4) AWS access key exposed in page source. (5) Rank #9 on Google Maps in own market. Art Plumbing is #8 on same street. Snyder is #2 with 29K reviews.

Composite Online Health Score: 53 out of 100. The biggest opportunity is fixing broken infrastructure, not more marketing.

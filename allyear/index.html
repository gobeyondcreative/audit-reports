Critical (Fix Immediately)

CriticalRanked #9 in the Coral Springs Map Pack

The 3-pack captures the large majority of local map clicks. At #9, a 52-year brand with 4.7 stars is effectively invisible at the moment of highest purchase intent. This is the downstream symptom of the citation, speed, and review-velocity problems below.

Fix: Treat NAP cleanup, page speed, and review velocity as one coordinated 90-day push. Map Pack rank is a lagging indicator. It moves when those three inputs move together, not individually.

Source: Boostcon App heatmap data

CriticalVerify published phone numbers and call routing

Phone numbers are published across Google Business Profile, the website header, the contact page, BBB, and Facebook. Google Maps and BBB share a primary number. No routing or attribution failure has been established.

Fix: Select one canonical NAP record. Route every channel through tracking numbers that forward to a single destination. Document which numbers are intentional tracking lines. Correct only listings confirmed to be wrong, then verify the changes.

Source: Boostcon App, BBB, Facebook, Website

CriticalMobile page speed: 18.2 seconds

The Boostcon App measured an 18.2-second mobile load time. Google's guidance is that mobile pages should be usable in under 3 seconds, so the site is roughly 6x slower than the recommended threshold, and the majority of mobile visitors leave before the page renders. This suppresses conversion rate and rankings at the same time. Visit the site on a phone to see the delay.

Fix: Compress and convert images to WebP, enable lazy loading, remove duplicated carousels, minify and defer non-critical CSS and JavaScript, and enable full-page caching plus a CDN. Re-test in PageSpeed Insights after each change so you know which fix produced which gain. This is the single highest-priority technical fix.

Source: Boostcon App PageSpeed data

CriticalCore Web Vitals near zero

Page Speed scored 0%, LCP 6%, and INP 6%, with a separate overall SEO score of 3%. Google uses these page-experience signals when deciding what to surface. Until they move, content and link work will underperform. The site is being held down in search results.

Fix: Run a focused technical SEO sprint. Improve LCP by optimizing hero images and reducing server response time. Improve INP by cutting main-thread JavaScript execution. Remove render-blocking resources. Validate against field data in Search Console, not just lab scores.

Source: Boostcon App Core Web Vitals data

CriticalNot ranking on Page 1 for core HVAC keywords

All Year Cooling is not ranking for AC repair Coral Springs, HVAC Coral Springs, or AC installation Coral Springs. Competitors own Page 1 while a 52-year-old brand is invisible in its home market.

Fix: Fix page speed first, since it gates everything else. Then optimize the existing Coral Springs landing page around one primary term, add supporting service pages, and build city-specific backlinks. Expect 3 to 6 months to Page 1.

Source: Google SERP analysis

CriticalReview volume is 4x to 11x lower than top competitors

All Year has ~2,580 Google reviews. Art Plumbing has 10,560 (~4x), and Snyder AC / Air America shows ~29,000 across all platforms (~11x). At current velocity, the gap will not close organically. Review count is a primary local ranking factor.

Fix: Deploy post-service SMS review requests within 24 hours of every completed job. Add GBP review links to technician follow-ups and email signatures. Target 50+ net new Google reviews per month and track velocity weekly, not monthly.

Source: Google, Birdeye-published competitor totals

HighTracking is installed but unverified

Google Ads, conversion tracking, GTM, GA4, and Meta Pixel are all present. Presence is not proof of accuracy. Call forwarding, conversion firing, and booked-job attribution have not been tested.

Fix: Run a tracking verification pass. Confirm each conversion fires once, deduplicates across GA4 and Google Ads, and reconciles against booked jobs in the field system. Anything that cannot be reconciled should be rebuilt, not trusted.

Source: Boostcon App tech stack scan

HighHours inconsistency: 24/7 on site, 7:30 AM to 10 PM in directories

The website claims 24/7 service. Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, and Angi show 7:30 AM to 10 PM. Conflicting hours cost after-hours calls and weaken Google's confidence in the business profile.

Fix: Confirm actual operating hours, then synchronize GBP, Yelp, BBB, Angi, and the website to match. If 24/7 is accurate, update all directories. If not, update the website before a customer discovers the gap at 11 PM.

Source: Website, Boostcon App, BBB, Yelp, Angi / HomeAdvisor

HighNo live chat or AI chat widget on website

No chat widget was found. Unanswered phones and missed callbacks are the most frequent complaint in the review sample, which means after-hours and hold-time drop-offs have nowhere to go. The site currently offers no alternate conversion path.

Fix: Deploy 24/7 AI chat that captures contact information, books appointments directly, and filters urgent vs. non-urgent requests. This addresses the top reputation complaint and the after-hours capture gap in one implementation.

Source: Boostcon App, Website, review themes

HighYelp and BBB ratings trail Google

The BBB sample is small enough that a handful of reviews move it significantly, but Yelp's 319-review base is not a small sample. Prospects comparing platforms will see the spread. Check Yelp and BBB.

Fix: Extend review requests beyond Google to Yelp and BBB. Respond to every negative review within 48 hours with a named owner and a resolution path. Fix the underlying operational issues driving the complaints, since review management alone will not hold the line.

Source: Yelp, BBB

HighRecurring negative theme: post-sale service and warranty breakdowns

Sentiment analysis shows 26% negative across a 50-review sample. Themes: phones not answered, callbacks not returned, warranty disputes, repeat unresolved repairs, no-shows, and communication failures after payment. Yelp reviews and BBB complaints confirm the pattern.

Fix: This is an operations problem surfacing as a marketing problem, and marketing cannot outrun it. Assign a single service-recovery owner. Add warranty claim tracking with an SLA. Automate post-job follow-up so nothing depends on someone remembering to call back.

Source: Boostcon App, Yelp, BBB

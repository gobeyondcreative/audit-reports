Methodology

Scoring: The 51% overall score and section scores are taken directly from the attached Sep 8, 2026 Boostcon App audit: Business Details 75%, Tech Stack 20%, Google Business Profile 100%, Listings 14%, Reputation 67%, Website Performance 64%, and Local SEO Heatmaps 16%. The report does not create a second custom weighting formula when the app already supplies the composite score.

Website performance discrepancy: The app's summary page reports Website Performance at 64%, while a detailed page reports 0% because the site was unreachable during that crawl. The website was reachable during this verification pass, so the detailed failure is treated as a point-in-time crawler or server event, not as proof that the site is currently offline.

Reputation counts: The app reputation module reports approximately 1.2K Google reviews at 4.9 stars. The app local rank tracker shows 1.29K. Current public directories can display different counts because of crawl timing and source lag. The report therefore uses approximately 1.2K to 1.29K instead of pretending one cached number is universal.

Tracking: The Boostcon App classifies Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion tracking, Google Tag Manager, and Google Analytics as unclear. Current website verification shows Google Tag Manager resources loading. Therefore the report does not claim those systems are absent. It recommends a manual implementation and event validation pass.

Revenue estimate: No specific lost-revenue figure is presented because the prior estimate relied partly on the incorrect assumption that the Google Business Profile was unclaimed. A defensible revenue leak estimate requires verified lead volume, close rate, average ticket, booked-job data, and source-level attribution.
